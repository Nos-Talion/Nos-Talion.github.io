I am currently a first-year PhD studnet in the [Yamakawa Laboratory](http://www.hfr.iis.u-tokyo.ac.jp/index-j.html) at the [The University of Tokyo](https://www.u-tokyo.ac.jp/en/), where I focus on developing intelligent systems for robotic manipulation. My research interests span across robot manipulation, robot design, Dynamics and Cybernetics, and reinforcement learning, with a growing emphasis on geometric deep learning and generative AI.

Feel free to reach out for collaboration and discussion of research ideas.
